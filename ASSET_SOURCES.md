# Asset sources

Vega's logo, fonts, Sam sprites, activity sprites, skyline layers, plants, floor tiles, controller controls, and lift frames are reused from the public Vega careers site for this assignment. `lib/assets.ts` records the original URLs and sprite geometry. These are Vega's assets; this project does not claim ownership of them.

The dedicated Sam dialogue portrait is from https://vega.io/assets/sprites/careers/portraits/sam.png. Costume variants are composed locally; activity guests use head crops from their matching original activity sprites. The three console station SVGs are original pixel drawings designed to share the orange Vega arcade's shallow oblique perspective. `lift-bay.svg` retains the original elevator frame and adds a shallow recess. Its surroundings are transparent so the shared wall, molding, and floor continue behind it; the original door sheet animates over the opening.

The supplied Figma guide defines Leaf Green #0D5500, Ink #0F3301, Daisy #FFFAEB, Yellow #FFD11B, and Sky #8DCAFF. PP Mondwest is used for pixel headings, dialogue, game menus, HUDs, and canvas text. Signifier is used for the schedule heading. Geist is used for body copy, and Geist Mono for page controls and keyboard hints. Sky Blue is reserved for backgrounds and sky illustrations. The user explicitly approved marine blue for the S-Cargo Yokohama Version livery; this is an illustration exception, not a new UI accent. The Vega wordmark retains its daisy.

The hall uses the author-supplied [Apple Cider loop](https://opengameart.org/content/apple-cider-juiced) by Zane Little, with quiet gain and gentle EQ for the requested Japanese life-sim mood. The four game scores are [Homely Arcade](https://opengameart.org/content/homely-arcade) by Spring Spring (Breaker), [Wacky Wobblings](https://opengameart.org/content/wacky-wobblings) by Fupi (Runner), [Fever Stadium](https://opengameart.org/content/fever-stadium) by MintoDog (Circuit), and [Welcome to the Item Shop](https://opengameart.org/content/welcome-to-the-item-shop) by congusbongus (Beat). These recordings are offered under CC0; Homely Arcade's CC0 option is selected. Exact source files, edits and licenses are recorded in `public/audio/manifest.json`, `public/audio/ATTRIBUTION.txt` and `tools/audio/`. The race track is an independent Eurobeat composition, not a recreation of Déjà Vu. Interface effects and the engine tone are synthesized in `lib/audio.ts`.

The Fiat 500 sheets are original pixel drawings based on the supplied Vega event photograph. They retain `PostSIEM.com` and the campaign livery, use a fictional 630 plate, and do not embed the photograph. The eleven rivals have their own silhouettes, rear/turn poses and complete left/right profiles. Ten use fictional SIEM bumper badges. S-Cargo carries only the requested 茶 plate, with blue sides and white vertical rear-edge strips. The blue approximates the supplied printed brochure; it is not a verified factory paint code. Code-native generators and geometry checks are in `tools/art/`.

The live hardware represents a 16-bit System 16-era arcade, Mega Drive/Genesis, SNES/Super Famicom, and Mega Drive + Mega-CD. The CD station is an add-on setup. Older NES/PS1 exploration assets remain as drafts and are not used by the live page. New device marks pair the arcade pixel wordmark with the Vega daisy.


NPC dialogue is fictional. References to Vega's real company locations and federated security analytics are linked in `lib/lore.ts`; they are not quotations attributed to employees.

The expanded wardrobe is composed over the original Sam frames in `lib/outfit-art.ts`. The original Vega dog is reused with normalized idle/run baselines. Cat, daisy fairy, bot and firefly companions are original four-frame pixel SVGs.

Movement timing and bounded square-pixel dust were studied again against the original Vega careers game. The venue adapts those principles to its own physics and lifecycle; original task gating, multiplayer and smoking behavior are not included. The scene keeps the original building silhouettes at a2x display scale. Game facades and advertisements reuse those textures and the supplied paired logo/daisy, with cached Canvas compositions. The car turn generator now projects a single rear face and joins the side profile without separately shearing the roof.
